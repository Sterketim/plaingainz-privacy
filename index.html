3. Information stored only on your device

You can choose to enter the following in the app. It is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, set types, workout times, and your optional rating and comment for a workout
  • Workout history, progression and personal records
  • Your training program and any custom program or workout
  • Profile details: name (optional), profile photo (optional), body weight and weight history, height, year of birth, sex, training goal, experience level and earned badges
  • Body measurements
  • Progress photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library
  • Daily activity level and calorie estimates calculated from the details above
  • Settings such as language and appearance
  • Drafts of Online Coach questions you have not yet sent

The app asks for access to the camera and photo library only so you can add progress photos and a profile photo. Photos stay on your device.

On Android, if backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your own Google backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we do not receive it and cannot access it.

This information is used only inside the app, for example to show your history and progress, to suggest weights and repetitions (Smart Coach, which runs on your device), and to estimate calories. It stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. We cannot access or restore it. Coaches never have access to it.

4. Online Coach

4.1 Age limit and consent

Online Coach is only for users aged 18 or older. Before you can send your first question, you tick two separate boxes in the app. Nothing is ticked in advance:

  • One where you confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • One where you confirm that you will not include health information and, if your question still contains such information, give your explicit consent to the coach reading it only to assess and, if needed, reject the question (section 4.3).

A link to this policy is shown next to the boxes. Our server stores when you confirmed and consented, and which version of the consent text you accepted, and it refuses new questions, new question purchases and new 12-week program orders (section 4.7) without them. If we change the consent text, you will be asked again. The age confirmation is a self-declaration; we do not verify your age.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app, just as easily as you gave it, without deleting your account (section 10a). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before you withdrew.

4.2 Signing in

To use Online Coach you sign in with Apple on iPhone or with Google on Android.

With Sign in with Apple, we do not request your name or email address from Apple, so we do not receive them. Apple gives us a unique identifier for your Apple account in this app, which we use to recognise your Online Coach account.

With Sign in with Google, Google gives us a unique identifier for your Google account, your email address and, if your Google account has them, your name and profile picture. We use the identifier to recognise your Online Coach account. The email address, name and profile picture are stored in our sign-in system only because Google provides them; we do not use them to contact you, and they are not shown in the coach view.

Our server stores an account identifier, the Apple or Google identifier linked to it (and, for Google, the email address, name and profile picture), and when the account was created and last signed in. A sign-in session is stored on your device so you stay signed in.

Our hosting providers record technical information such as IP address, time and type of request in server logs and sign-in audit records, for security and to operate the service (see section 9 for how long).

4.3 Questions, answers and health information

Online Coach gives general guidance on strength training, muscle growth, training programs, exercise technique, load and progression, and general nutrition guidance related to training and building muscle (for example eating habits, protein and calories). It does not give medical advice, diagnoses, injury treatment, medical nutrition therapy or advice about illness or other health problems, and it does not replace a doctor, clinical dietitian or other health professional. The app shows this next to the question field and asks you not to include information about illnesses, injuries, allergies, eating disorders, medication, pregnancy or other health information.

When you send a question, we store:

  • The text of your question and the coach you chose
  • The status of the question (for example awaiting payment, paid, answered, rejected or expired) and the dates it was created, paid, answered, rejected or expired
  • The coach's answer

If a question still contains health information (special category data under the GDPR), the coach reads it only to assess it, based on your explicit consent (section 4.1). A question that requires a medical assessment (for example health, illness, injuries or medical nutrition) is rejected: its text is deleted immediately, the question is closed without an answer, and you receive one credit for a new question. You can see in the app that the question was rejected and why. If you would rather have your money back, you can ask Apple or Google Play, wherever you bought it, for a refund.

If a paid question is not answered in time, it expires and you receive one credit for a new question. The app shows how many credits you have, and which questions were paid with a credit. If a purchase is refunded by Apple or Google Play, the credit linked to it is cancelled. If you have received an answer to your question, you keep the answer even if the purchase is later refunded.

You can delete a question that has not been paid for at any time in the app under My questions. Unpaid questions are deleted automatically 30 days after they were created. Paid questions, purchases and credits are never deleted this way.

Each purchase attempt gets its own number. We store when the attempt started, whether it was cancelled, what your phone reports about the purchase and, if relevant, a one-way hash of the transaction number. If a question is stuck on an unclear purchase, support can check the purchase with RevenueCat and release the question. We then store the number of purchases the check found, who assessed the case, the reason and the time. The question text is not included, and a release never records anything as paid.

Each coach signs in to the coach panel with their own account and two-step sign-in, and sees only the questions sent to them. The coach reads your question to answer it; if it contains health information, the coach reads that only to assess and reject the question. As data controller, Tim Enersen can also see questions sent to other coaches, only when needed for customer support, payment checks or rejection. No other people have access; our service providers process the data only as described in section 6. If other coaches are added later, we will update this policy before they get access.

In the coach view, only paid questions are shown: the question text, its status and dates, whether the payment was a real purchase or a test purchase (Apple or Google Play), and the answer. The coach view does not show your name, email address, Apple ID, Google account, purchase details or any information stored on your device, including your training history. The question is linked only to a pseudonymous account number that does not reveal who you are.

In the administration panel, the data controller can also see unpaid questions and payment status. This is used only to run Online Coach, handle support and payments, and meet legal obligations. The coach panel is protected by password and two-step sign-in.

4.5 Purchases of questions and Premium check

Online Coach requires an active Plain Gainz Premium subscription, and each question is a separate one-time in-app purchase through Apple or Google Play. To confirm a payment, our server checks the purchase with RevenueCat and stores the transaction identifier, product, purchase date, whether it was a real or test (sandbox) purchase, and any refund. To confirm Premium, our server checks with RevenueCat whether your subscription is active and stores the result and expiry date.

4.6 Notifications

You can choose to get a notification on your phone when the coach has answered, and if the coach cannot answer your question because it is outside what Online Coach advises on. The app only asks for permission when you tap "Turn on notifications", and Online Coach works the same without notifications: the answer is always shown under My questions.

If you turn notifications on, we store a notification address for your phone (an Expo push token) and the app language, linked to your Online Coach account. The notification only says that the answer is ready (with the name of the coach who answered, for example "Maria Brandstadmoen has answered your question"), or that the question could not be answered and that you have received one credit, and contains the question's internal number so the app can open it. It never contains your question, the answer, the reason for rejecting it, your name or any health information. A notification about a rejected question is only sent while the credit is valid. The notification is sent through Expo's push service and, on iPhone, Apple Push Notification service or, on Android, Firebase Cloud Messaging from Google.

The notification address is deleted from our server when you sign out of Online Coach, delete your Online Coach account or withdraw your consent, when the push service reports that it no longer works, and in any case after 180 days without use. You can also turn notifications off at any time in the iPhone Settings app or in the notification settings on your Android device.

4.7 Personal 12-week program from a coach

Applies from Android version 1.2.5 and iPhone version 1.2.0. How quickly the program is delivered is set out in the terms of purchase.

You can order a personal 12-week program as a separate in-app purchase. It requires your Online Coach account (section 4.2), the confirmations in section 4.1, an active Premium subscription, and that you are 18 or older. When you order, we store your answers in the order form: age, goal, training experience, how active you are in daily life, whether you can walk briskly for 20 minutes, how many days and how long you want to train, where you train and what equipment you have, and, if you choose, gender, height, weight, current strength level and exercises you want to avoid. We also store the program type, any self-declarations you confirm in the form, the messages in the conversation about the order, the order status and the program itself. If you tick the box for it, we send one reminder if the payment is not completed.

**One common approach for all program types.** You choose one of four program types: "Get in shape in 12 weeks", "Strong Mom – 12 Weeks", "12-week home training" or "12-week 30-minute program". In the App Store and Google Play, each program type has its own price. Everything in this section applies equally to all four: the same information, the same health consent and the same rules on who can see the information, storage, deletion and withdrawal. Some program types ask fewer questions about place and equipment, but no program type has its own health questions – not even "Strong Mom – 12 Weeks".

**Program type.** The type is stored on the order and in the program so that the coach can create, deliver and follow up the right program. The program type is a product choice and is not in itself health information. It is used only to create, deliver and follow up the program, is seen only by the same people as your answers (below), and is not sent to RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, Expo or the ad platforms. Each program type has its own purchase product with a neutral product ID that does not reveal the program type. RevenueCat receives the purchase to confirm payment, but purchases of 12-week programs are not forwarded from RevenueCat to AppsFlyer or the advertising platforms.

**Self-declarations.** Some program types require you to confirm a self-declaration before you can order, for example that you train at your own risk (see the terms of purchase). We store on the order that you confirmed, the time and which version of the text you confirmed, as documentation. The declaration contains no information you write yourself.

**Health information.** Whether you can get down to the floor and up again, problems with knees, back, shoulders or hips, and limitations you describe yourself are health information (special categories under GDPR Article 9). These fields are optional and are processed only if you tick the box for explicit consent in the form (the health consent). The box is not ticked in advance, and it is the same health consent for all program types. If you skip the fields, the coach assumes you can train on the floor and have no problems.

**"Exercises you want to avoid".** This is an optional, ordinary field for all program types. Just write the exercise, not the reason – the form says this too. Do not write health information here (for example injuries, illness, pain, pregnancy or childbirth); use the health fields above if you want the coach to take such things into account. If you still write health information in the field, it is protected like the health fields: it is seen only by the coach you chose and an authorised administrator, only after the order has been paid, every view is logged, and it is deleted together with the health information (below).

**Who can see the information.** Only the coach the order is assigned to (section 4.4) and the controller acting as administrator can see it. The coach may be a different trainer who creates programs for us than the one you chose; that trainer is then our data processor, with a data processing agreement in place before access (section 6). Reminders about the delivery deadline are internal: they go only to the coach and the administrator and contain none of your answers. Both sign in to the coach panel with two-step sign-in. The health fields and the "Exercises you want to avoid" field are shown only after the order has been paid, and every view is logged. If the order is moved to another coach, the previous coach loses access. Your answers and the program are never sent to RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, Expo or the ad platforms. Notifications about the program only say that it is ready, has been updated, or that there is a new message.

**Deletion.** The health information and the "Exercises you want to avoid" field are deleted once the coach has delivered the program and marked it as finished with no adjustment open, and no later than 30 days after delivery. They are deleted immediately if you are refunded, cancel the order or withdraw your consent.

**Withdrawing your health consent.** You can withdraw your health consent at any time in the app, without cancelling the order:

  • Online Coach → My orders → "Withdraw health consent"
  • In "Order conversation" → "Withdraw health consent"
  • Online Coach → "Withdraw consent" (section 10a). This also stops new questions and new program orders until you consent again
  • By deleting your Online Coach account (section 10), or by emailing us (section 11)

The health information and the "Exercises you want to avoid" field are then deleted immediately from all your orders, and the app also deletes them from order drafts on your phone. The coach creates or adjusts the program without them. The order, the payment, the program type and the self-declaration are not affected and are kept as described in section 9. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before you withdrew. The training log from the program is stored only on your phone, as described in section 3.

5. Subscriptions, free trial and purchases

Premium subscriptions, question purchases and the 12-week program are bought through the Apple App Store on iPhone, where Apple processes the payment, and through Google Play on Android, where Google processes the payment. We do not receive or store payment card details.

Any free trial is offered and managed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play, and the app shows it only when the store offers it to you. If you have allowed notifications, the app also shows a reminder the day before and on the day the store's free trial ends, so you can cancel before the first payment is taken. If you have already cancelled the trial, you may also get a reminder the day after. The reminders are created locally on your phone and can be turned off under Settings → Notifications. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings or, on Android, in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

**The in-app free period (Android).** New users on Android can start 7 days free in the app themselves, without a payment card and without a subscription. The free period does not renew and never turns into a payment; when it ends, you decide whether to buy a subscription in Google Play. If you have allowed notifications, the app shows up to three reminders: the day before the free period ends, the day it ends and the day after. The reminders are created and shown locally on your phone and send no information to us or anyone else. You can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings → Notifications, or in your phone's notification settings. To give each phone only one free period, the app then sends a device identifier for the app (Android's ANDROID_ID) and the anonymous app user ID from RevenueCat to our server. The server never stores the Android ID in plain text, only a pseudonymised value computed from it with HMAC-SHA256 and a secret key, together with the start and end time and the app user ID. If you have had a subscription or Google's free trial before, no new free period is given. This information is used only to grant and limit the free period. The legal basis is our agreement with you (giving you the free period you ask for) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. Your training data is never sent.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases. When the app contacts RevenueCat, its software in the app sends purchase and subscription information (such as which products you have bought and whether your subscription is active), an anonymous app user ID, and technical information about your device and the app. This includes the device model, operating system version, language settings, App Store country, app version, whether tracking is allowed on your device (from version 1.2.0 the app asks for this permission only if you have first allowed ad measurement, section 5a; earlier versions never ask), and Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier for your device that is shared by apps from the same developer. Like any server, RevenueCat also sees the IP address of the connection. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) unless you have allowed both ad measurement in the app and tracking in Apple's prompt (section 5a).

On Android, RevenueCat's software sends your purchase history and subscription status, an anonymous app user ID and technical information about the app and your device. The Apple identifiers above are not used on Android. We do not use Google's advertising ID or collect advertising identifiers through RevenueCat, unless you allow ad measurement (section 5a).

RevenueCat also shows us statistics about purchases and subscriptions, such as a customer's purchase history and overall figures. We use this to run and understand the subscription service and, on Android, to see which of our ads and links lead to trials and subscriptions (below and section 5a). We do not use it to show you ads.

When you use Online Coach, your Online Coach account identifier is also stored with RevenueCat so our server can confirm that the purchases belong to your account. It is removed when you delete your Online Coach account.

Where you installed the app from (Android)

On Android, the first time you open the app, it reads the install referrer that Google Play provides for the installation. This applies to all Android users, without a prompt, and does not use the advertising ID. From it, the app keeps only short, cleaned labels: the channel (for example tiktok, facebook, instagram, snapchat, google_ads or organic), the medium, the campaign, the ad group and the ad. Each label is at most 64 characters and contains only a–z, 0–9 and _ . -. The app never stores the full referrer text or Google's click identifier (gclid).

The labels are stored as attributes on your anonymous RevenueCat customer, so we can see which of our ads and links lead to free trials and subscriptions. They are not shared with ad networks.

5a. Measuring our ads (iPhone and Android, only if you allow it)

Ad measurement is available on Android from version 1.2.0 and on iPhone from version 1.2.0. Earlier versions do not measure our ads with AppsFlyer. After the first setup, the app asks once on the Home screen whether we may measure our ads. "Allow" and "Don't allow" are equally easy to choose, and closing the question counts as no. You can change your answer at any time under Profile → Settings → Ad measurement. The app works the same whether you allow it or not. Your answer is stored on your device.

If you do not allow it, the AppsFlyer software in the app is not started and nothing is sent to AppsFlyer. On iPhone, the app then does not show Apple's tracking prompt either.

On iPhone, the app shows Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency) only after you have allowed ad measurement in the app. On iPhone, AppsFlyer is started only if you said yes in the app AND allow tracking in Apple's prompt; AppsFlyer may then use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline Apple's prompt (or tracking is restricted), AppsFlyer is not started and nothing is sent to AppsFlyer. You can change your answer to Apple's prompt at any time in your iPhone settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

From Android 1.2.1 and iPhone 1.2.0, the following also applies: some events happen before you are asked (completing the first setup, the program preview, the subscription screen and, on Android, starting the in-app free trial). These are stored only on your device. If you allow ad measurement, they are sent to AppsFlyer once. If you decline, they are deleted without being sent.

From Android 1.2.2 and iPhone 1.2.0 (Build 28), one event is also sent when you complete your first workout in the app. The event only says that the first workout was completed: no exercises, weights, repetitions, duration or other content from the workout. It is sent at most once, never if you already had saved workouts, and under the same rules as above (stored only on your device until you answer, sent after a yes, deleted after a no).

If you allow it:

  • AppsFlyer Ltd. (Israel) receives device identifiers as collected by the AppsFlyer software (on Android Google's advertising ID, on iPhone Apple's identifier for vendor, IDFV, and the IDFA), that the app was installed and opened, that you completed the first setup, saw the program preview and the subscription screen, and on Android that you started the in-app free trial, that you completed your first workout (from Android 1.2.2 and iPhone 1.2.0 Build 28, without any content from the workout), together with basic device information (model, operating system version, language and IP address). No names, free text or training data.
  • AppsFlyer uses the IP address to work out your approximate location (country, region and city). The app never asks for access to your location and does not collect precise (GPS) location. The approximate location is used to measure our ads and may be included in the result passed to the ad platforms (below).
  • Through RevenueCat's AppsFlyer integration, AppsFlyer also receives subscription events: first purchase, the start, conversion and cancellation of a store trial, renewal, cancellation, expiration, billing issues and subscription changes, with product, price and currency. One-time purchases, that is, purchases of Online Coach questions and of 12-week programs, are not forwarded from RevenueCat to AppsFlyer or the advertising platforms. The start of the in-app free trial (Android) is sent by the app itself; RevenueCat may also send when the free trial ends. The free trial is never reported to the ad platforms as a paid subscription.
  • RevenueCat receives your AppsFlyer ID and your device's advertising ID, along with other device identifiers collected by RevenueCat's software for this purpose.
  • AppsFlyer passes the result, that is, which ad led to the installation, trial or subscription, to the ad platform whose ad you saw (TikTok, Meta for Facebook and Instagram, Snap or Google, for Google Ads), so it can measure and improve our campaigns. The result may include the advertising ID or AppsFlyer ID, the event, the time, the IP address and the approximate location. These platforms process this information under their own terms.
  • The ad source AppsFlyer finds (media source, campaign, ad set and ad) is stored once on your RevenueCat customer and may replace the labels from Google Play (section 5).

On Android, the AppsFlyer software also asks the Facebook and Instagram apps on your phone (Meta Install Referrer) whether a Meta ad led to the installation. This happens only when AppsFlyer has been started, that is, after you have allowed ad measurement in the app.

On iPhone, the ad platforms also use Apple's SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit. iOS itself then tells the ad platform that an ad led to an installation, with a delay and without identifiers for you or your device. This is controlled by Apple and does not require tracking permission. When AppsFlyer is not started (no in the app or in Apple's prompt), the app itself tells iOS once that the app was installed, so that this anonymous measurement works. No identifiers, events or information about you are sent from the app in that case.

If you had the app before version 1.2.0, AppsFlyer reports you as an update, not a new installation.

If you later withdraw your answer, the app tells AppsFlyer that consent has been withdrawn, stops the AppsFlyer software, and removes the AppsFlyer ID from your RevenueCat customer so RevenueCat stops forwarding events. The advertising ID and IP address RevenueCat received while you allowed it remain on your RevenueCat customer, but are not used for ad measurement without the AppsFlyer ID. You can ask us to delete them, and we will then do so in RevenueCat (section 11). Information already sent stays with AppsFlyer for its retention period (section 9). You can ask us to have it deleted, and we will then send a deletion request to AppsFlyer (section 11). Information the ad platforms have already received is processed under their own terms, and you can also ask them directly to delete it.

Your training data, workouts, body measurements, photos, Online Coach questions, order form answers and health information are never shared with AppsFlyer or the ad platforms. The app shows no ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, or turn off tracking for the app in your iPhone settings.
