Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Plain Gainz is a strength training app for logging workouts and following your progress. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and who it is shared with.

In short: your training data stays on your device, the app does not require an account, and we do not receive your payment card details.

Information you enter in the app

You can choose to save the following in Plain Gainz:

  • Workouts, including exercises, sets, weights and repetitions
  • Workout history, progression and personal records
  • Your selected training program
  • Profile details: name (optional), body weight and training goal
  • Your language setting

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot access it. Body weight and training data are only used inside the app to show your own history and progress.

No account required

Plain Gainz does not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your email address or phone number through the app.

Free trial

The app includes a 7-day free trial that does not require payment or payment details. The start date of the trial is stored locally on your device so the app can tell when the trial has ended.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. Plain Gainz does not receive or store your payment card information.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. To do this, RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription information, such as which subscription you have and whether it is active, together with technical identifiers needed to manage the subscription, such as an anonymous app user ID and basic device and app information. This is used to confirm your subscription and give you access to the app.

You can read more in Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

How information is used

  • To provide the app’s features, such as workout logging, history and progression
  • To keep track of your free trial
  • To manage your subscription and confirm that it is active

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Because your training data is stored on your device, it remains there until you delete it. Deleting the app from your device removes the data stored by the app on that device. Please note that this cannot be undone, and we cannot restore the data for you.

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings, where you can view or cancel a subscription at any time.

Your rights and questions

You can contact us with any questions about privacy, about the information handled in connection with your subscription, or to ask for that information to be deleted. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you.

Children

Plain Gainz is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or the way it handles information changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
