Part A – Using the app and Premium

Plain Gainz is a training app. On Android, the Premium subscription is bought, renewed and cancelled in Google Play, and Google's terms apply to the payment. On iPhone, Premium is bought in the App Store, and Apple's standard licence agreement (EULA) and Apple's terms apply. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in the store. Please also read the privacy policy: https://sterketim.github.io/plaingainz-privacy/

The training content in the app is general training guidance, not medical treatment or rehabilitation.

Part B – Personal 12-week program (purchase terms)

0. Seller. Seller: TIM ENERSEN (sole proprietorship), org. no. 922660476, Heliveien 2, 1820 Spydeberg, Norway, email plaingainz.norge@gmail.com. On Android, the app is published on Google Play under the developer name Nordic Gainz. On iPhone, the app is published on the App Store by TIM ENERSEN.

1. What you buy. A personal 12-week training program. You choose one of four program types when you order: "Get in shape in 12 weeks", "Strong Mom – 12 Weeks", "12-week home training" or "12-week 30-minute program". The same terms apply to all four. The program is set up from your answers using our program templates, and a coach personally reviews and adapts every program before it is delivered. The coach may be a different trainer who creates programs for us than the one you chose; we remain responsible to you, and your price and rights are the same. The program is added to your account in the Plain Gainz app.

2. Price. Each program type has its own price. The price shown in the app and in the App Store or Google Play before you pay, in your local currency, is the final price. Any taxes are handled by Apple or Google. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it does not renew. The program is bought in addition to Premium and is not included in the subscription.

3. Who can buy. You need an active Premium subscription (monthly or yearly), also if you are in the free trial in the App Store or Google Play. Free access from a promotion does not include buying. You must be 18 or older.

4. Delivery. The program is normally delivered within 24 hours, and no later than 48 hours, after confirmed payment. For orders placed in the Android app version 1.2.5, the program is delivered within 5 business days after confirmed payment. The deadline that applies to your order, with the date, is stated in the confirmation in the order conversation. If it takes longer, we tell you in the order conversation. You can then demand delivery, or cancel the purchase and get your money back, and you have the other rights the Norwegian Digital Services Act (digitalytelsesloven) gives you in case of delay.

5. Order conversation. Questions about the order and the program you ordered (price, ordering, delivery, content, status and adjustments) are free in the order conversation. Other training questions are asked as regular Online Coach questions.

6. Right of withdrawal. You have a 14-day right of withdrawal from when you paid, even after the program has been delivered. How to cancel: write "I cancel my purchase" in the order conversation, or email us at plaingainz.norge@gmail.com. You can also use the withdrawal form below. You will get the full amount back within 14 days. When you order, you ask the coach to start right away; you still keep your right of withdrawal.

7. Refunds. Refunds are handled manually, under the rules of the store you bought from and consumer law. There are no automatic refunds. Bought on Android: we refund in Google Play. Bought on iPhone: Apple refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com, and we will help you. Once the purchase is refunded, the program is removed from your account. You keep the sessions you have logged.

8. When Premium ends. The program you bought and your progress are saved – nothing is deleted. To use the program (view the sessions, follow them and log), Premium must be active. When you activate Premium again, you continue in the same week with the same sessions.

9. Health. All program types are general training guidance, not medical treatment or rehabilitation. If you have an injury or illness, or are pregnant, talk to a doctor or physiotherapist before you start. Stop if you feel pain.

9a. "Strong Mom – 12 Weeks". A strength program for mothers who want to get stronger – in everyday life, for themselves and their family. This is a general training program, not medical treatment or rehabilitation. Before you can order it, you must confirm that you train at your own risk. If you are unsure whether you can train, talk to a doctor or another health professional first. Stop training and contact a health professional if you get pain or other unusual symptoms. The program has no health questions of its own; the same optional health consent applies as for the other program types (section 10).

10. Privacy. See section 4.7 of the privacy policy. The same privacy rules and the same health consent apply to all four program types. Health information is optional, requires separate consent, is seen only by your coach and an authorised administrator, and is deleted no later than 30 days after delivery. Your form answers and health information are never sent to ad platforms. In the "Exercises you want to avoid" field, just write the exercise, not the reason – do not write health information there. You can withdraw your health consent at any time in the app: under "My orders" ("Withdraw health consent"), in "Order conversation" ("Withdraw health consent") or under Online Coach → "Withdraw consent". The health information and the "Exercises you want to avoid" field are then deleted immediately; the order and the program are not affected.

11. Training log. Your training log is stored on your phone. The program is on your account and can be downloaded to a new phone after you sign in. The log from a previous phone does not come along.

12. Contact and complaints. Use the order conversation or email plaingainz.norge@gmail.com. The Norwegian Consumer Council (Forbrukerrådet) can help if we cannot agree.

13. Defects. If the program is not as agreed, you can complain in the order conversation. We will fix it free of charge, and if it cannot be fixed, you will get your money back. You have the rights the law gives you.
